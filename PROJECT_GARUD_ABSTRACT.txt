ABSTRACT SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Modern enterprise networks operate under constant, asymmetric warfare. Advanced 
persistent threats (APTs) and sophisticated cyber adversaries rarely strike in 
single isolated bursts; instead, they conduct calculated multi-stage campaigns—
silently probing perimeters, establishing stealthy initial access, propagating 
laterally, and exfiltrating intellectual property long before traditional defenses 
detect their presence. Conventional Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) and Security 
Information and Event Management (SIEM) architectures are fundamentally reactive, 
alerting security analysts only after an intrusion has compromised critical 
infrastructure. Project Garud—inspired by the mythic avian guardian celebrated for 
its panoramic, telescopic vision and lightning reflexes—redefines defensive cyber 
operations by flipping the paradigm from retrospective post-mortems to proactive, 
real-time attack forecasting. Rather than asking whether an attack is underway, 
Garud continuously predicts where the adversary will advance within the next one to 
four minutes.

At the core of Project Garud is a novel dual-scale dynamical system engine:

First, at the microscopic flow resolution, Garud continuously tracks active network 
sessions across rolling temporal windows of 6 flows parameterized by 22 bidirectional 
traffic kinetics, including packet inter-arrival distributions, payload asymmetries, 
and TCP flag transitions. A deep multi-head Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) network 
projects next-state feature regressions and classifies the session's evolving 
adversarial trajectory across the MITRE ATT&CK kill chain—from Reconnaissance and 
Initial Access through Lateral Movement, Command & Control (C2), and Exfiltration—
enhanced by batched Monte Carlo simulations with stochastic noise perturbation to 
quantify predictive uncertainty bounds.

Second, at the macroscopic network resolution, Garud abstracts millions of raw 
transfers into unified 45-dimensional network state vectors calculated every sixty 
seconds. These state vectors encode port fan-outs, connection rates, protocol 
distributions, and communication entropies without geographic or IP-directional bias. 
A secondary temporal World Model LSTM rolls this global state vector forward across 
1, 2, 3, and 4-minute horizons, implementing a calibrated sustained-threat 
threshold rule (0.518 sustained over five consecutive minutes) to forecast multi-host 
saturation and coordinate attacks before network degradation occurs.

Operating in parallel with the sequence forecasters is a high-throughput 
HistGradientBoosting flow classifier trained across 2.0 million flows from benchmark 
datasets. Capable of evaluating over 15,000 flows per second, this engine categorizes 
traffic across nine granular behavioral classes—including Port Scans, Brute Force, 
Denial-of-Service, DDoS, Web Attacks, Botnet activity, Infiltration, and Heartbleed 
signature matches.

To ensure operational trust in life-critical environments, Garud replaces black-box 
heuristics with glass-box Explainable AI (XAI). Every forecast and alert is instantly 
deconstructed via SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) KernelExplainers and neural 
gradient attributions, exposing the exact microsecond packet inter-arrival delta or 
flag anomaly driving the risk score. For forensic integrity and legal chain-of-custody, 
all alerts and operator actions are cryptographically sealed into a SHA-256 hash-chained 
ledger that provides mathematical proof against log tampering. Threat containment is 
simultaneously autonomous and actionable: Garud dynamically synthesizes ready-to-execute 
Linux iptables scripts and JSON SOAR payloads to quarantine malicious endpoints at the 
firewall boundary the moment a predicted trajectory crosses mitigation thresholds.

The platform includes an air-gapped, zero-contamination offline analysis sandbox that 
parses multi-gigabyte PCAP/PCAPNG streams, Zeek logs, Suricata eve.json, and custom 
CSV tables in memory, automatically normalizing heterogeneous schemas and back-testing 
predictive temporal accuracy against historical ground truth without contaminating live 
production state. Packaged within a retro-technical, high-density React mission control 
console featuring live 3D GeoIP topology mapping and real-time statistical feature drift 
monitoring, Project Garud solves the defender's fundamental dilemma—delivering the 
ultimate tactical advantage in high-stakes cyber defense: the power to preempt the future.
